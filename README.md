# KCD Valencia Organizer Roles

This document describes the core organizer roles for KCD Valencia and the responsibilities owned by each.

## Finance Role

Owns the finances of KCD Valencia.

- Creating and maintaining the event budget
- Communicating with the fiscal sponsor
- Tracking the current state of the account.

## Session Role

Owns speaker selection and communication.

- Setting up Sessionize with the Call for Proposals (CFP)
- Reviewing and selecting sessions with the program committee
- All communication with speakers, from CFP through the event.

## Marketing Role

Owns marketing and promotion of KCD Valencia.

- Managing social media accounts and posts
- Creating promotional materials and graphics
- Coordinating with media partners
- Writing and distributing event announcements

## Venue Role

Owns venue selection and logistics.

- Sourcing and negotiating with venue providers
- Coordinating catering and on-site logistics
- Managing AV and technical setup requirements
- Ensuring accessibility and safety compliance

## Sponsor Role

Owns sponsor acquisition and relationships.

- Identifying and reaching out to potential sponsors
- Managing sponsorship packages and agreements
- Ensuring sponsor deliverables are fulfilled
- Maintaining ongoing sponsor relationships

## Volunteer Role

Owns volunteer recruitment and coordination.

- Recruiting and onboarding volunteers
- Scheduling volunteer shifts for the event
- Providing volunteer training and instructions
- Coordinating day-of volunteer logistics
